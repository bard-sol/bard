Straight answers

The questions teams ask in the first five minutes.

Do you take a percentage of my token?

No. Not at onboarding, not from the reward pool, not later. Bard is paid in SOL at the prices listed above, and nowhere else.

Do I need to hold a Bard token to use this?

No. The platform stands on its own. If a Bard token ever exists, it will be separate — using the platform will never depend on it.

Who decides who qualifies?

The rule you publish. Hold-style rules can be checked on-chain. Other campaign types use the same public leaderboard and claim flow so results aren’t hidden in a private chat.

What if a campaign flops?

You’re out the campaign fee. Claim fees only apply when rewards are actually claimed, so a campaign nobody finishes costs almost nothing.

Is any of it public?

Rankings and settled claims are meant to be. That’s the point of a shared record — the community can see what was earned without asking for a screenshot.
