# Trial content lock

Base HP is 115. Combo bonus is +4. A chapter 1 plain day has 115 boss HP.

Grenna Ironvow, Silas Quill, Captain Vey, Thorn, and Ash & Ember stay on their previous signatures.

- Lady Marrow, Standing Ovation: Deal 8. +3 more per Combo spent, on top of the normal Combo bonus.
- Pip Fennick, Heads I Win: Deal 9. An odd turn doubles it. While the boss is already in Finisher the deal is tripled instead.
- Old Wick, Candle Hex: the base tick is 3 each turn end, and it stacks. During Finisher each stack ticks for a flat 6, which replaces the old ×1.5. The opening tick of 1 stays 1.

The Hob + Wren check is greedy median 50–70, a lookahead gap of at least 15, and lookahead kills of 18–32%.

Ally nerfs, with the rest of the ally cards unchanged:

- Baron Croak, Royal Leap: Deal 5. Keep half your Combo (floor) instead of resetting.
- Grub, Moss Wall: Block 9. +1 Combo.
