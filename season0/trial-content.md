# Trial content lock

Base HP is 115. Combo bonus is +4. A chapter 1 plain day has 115 boss HP.

Grenna Ironvow, Silas Quill, Captain Vey, Thorn, and Ash & Ember stay on their previous signatures.

- Lady Marrow, Standing Ovation: Deal 8. +3 more per Combo spent, on top of the normal Combo bonus.
- Pip Fennick, Heads I Win: Deal 9. An odd turn doubles it. While the boss is already in Finisher the deal is tripled instead.
- Old Wick, Candle Hex: the base tick is 3 each turn end, and it stacks. During Finisher each stack ticks for a flat 6, which replaces the old ×1.5. The opening tick of 1 stays 1.

The Hob + Wren check is greedy median 50–70, a lookahead gap of at least 15, and lookahead kills of 18–32%.

Ally rule: no ally's average kills, across every pair it appears in, may sit more than about 8 points above the Hob + Wren pair. Hob + Wren itself was not changed.

Final ally cards:

- Hob, Stitch Up: Block 6. Keep 3 Block into next turn.
- Sister Wren, Lamplight: +1 Combo. Reveal the boss's next two intents.
- Juniper, Pick the Lock: +1 Combo, once per turn. Boss loses 6 guard.
- Sir Footnote, Tiny Charge: Deal 3. Deal 7 if the boss is Staggered.
- Old Mother Index, Cross-Reference: Draw 1. +1 Combo if it's an Attack, once per turn.
- Baron Croak, Royal Leap: Deal 3. Keep half your Combo (floor) instead of resetting.
- Mira, Mark the Map: Next Attack deals +4.
- Brannoc, Toll Paid: Block 7. Full block: draw 1 next turn.
- Lumen, Dusk Swarm: Deal 2 three times. Combo applies to the first hit only.
- Grub, Moss Wall: 2 energy. Gain 8 block. No Combo.
- Tilde, Hedge Hex: the boss's next attack deals 20% less (floor).
- Corvin, Urgent Delivery: Deal 3. During Finisher, deal 9 instead.
