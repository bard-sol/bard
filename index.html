Straight answers

The questions every team asks in the first five minutes.

Do you take a percentage of my token?

No. Not at onboarding, not from the reward pool, not later. Bard is paid in SOL at the prices listed above, and nowhere else.

Do I need to hold a Bard token to use this?

No. The platform stands on its own. If a Bard token ever exists, it will be a separate thing — using the platform will never depend on it.

Who decides which members qualify?

The rule does. You write the condition, Bard checks it, and the leaderboard shows the result. No moderator judging who was loyal enough.

What if a campaign flops?

You're out 0.25 SOL. Claim fees only apply to rewards that actually settle, so a campaign nobody finishes costs almost nothing.

Is any of it public?

Rankings and settled payouts are. That's the point of a record — your community can verify what was earned without asking you for a screenshot.

A settled reward gets the smile.

That's all the mark means, and it's why it's worth something: it appears when work was verified and a reward was actually paid. Collect enough of them and the record speaks for itself.
