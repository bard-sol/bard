Straight answers

The questions every team asks in the first five minutes.

Do you take a percentage of my token?

No. Not at onboarding, not from the reward pool, not later. Bard is paid in SOL at the prices listed above and nowhere else.

Do I need to hold a $BARD token to use this?

No. The platform ships first and stands on its own. Any future $BARD meme is a separate entity — using Bard will never depend on its chart.

Who decides which wallets qualify?

The rule does. You write the condition, Bard checks it, and the leaderboard shows the result. No mod judging who was loyal enough.

What if the campaign flops?

You're out 0.25 SOL. Claim fees only apply to rewards that actually settle, so a campaign nobody finishes costs you almost nothing.

Is any of it public?

Rank and settled payouts are. That's the whole point of a record — your community can verify what was earned without asking you for a screenshot.

A settled row gets the smile.

That's all the mark means, and it's the reason it's worth anything: it shows up when a deed was verified and a reward actually moved. Collect enough of them and the record speaks for itself.
