Straight answers

The questions everyone asks in the first five minutes.

Do you take a percentage of my token?

No. Not at onboarding, not from the reward pool, not later. Bard is paid in SOL at the prices listed above, and nowhere else.

Do I need to hold a Bard token to use this?

No. The platform stands on its own. If a Bard token ever exists, it will be a separate thing — using the platform will never depend on it.

Who decides who gets paid?

The challenge does. The team writes the goal, Bard checks it, and the leaderboard shows the result. No moderator judging who was loyal enough.

What if a campaign flops?

You're out 0.25 SOL. Payout fees only apply to rewards that are actually paid, so a campaign nobody finishes costs almost nothing.

Is any of it public?

Rankings and payouts are. That's the point — your community can see what was earned without asking anyone for a screenshot.

The smile means it was paid.

That's all the mark means, and it's why it's worth something: it appears when work was checked and a reward actually moved. Collect enough of them and the record speaks for itself.
